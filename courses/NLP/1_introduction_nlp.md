## Introduction

Natural Language Processing (NLP) is one of the hottest areas of artificial intelligence (AI) thanks to applications like text generators, chatbots, and text-to-image programs. Recent years have revolutionized the ability of computers to understand human languages, programming languages, and even biological sequences like DNA and proteins. The latest AI models analyze the meanings of input text and generate meaningful, expressive output.

---

## What is Natural Language Processing (NLP)?

NLP is the discipline of building machines that can manipulate human language. It evolved from computational linguistics but is focused on engineering technology to accomplish useful tasks.

Two main subfields:
- **Natural Language Understanding (NLU):** Semantic analysis to determine meaning.
- **Natural Language Generation (NLG):** Machine-generated text.

NLP often works with speech recognition but is distinct from it.

---

## Why Does NLP Matter?

- Everyday life applications: customer service chatbots, electronic health record analysis, conversational agents like Alexa and Siri.
- Google uses NLP to improve search.
- Social networks apply it for hate speech detection.
- Challenges: bias, incoherence, erratic behavior.
- Opportunities: expanding societal applications.

---

## What is NLP Used For?

NLP supports a wide range of tasks:

1. **Sentiment Analysis** – Classify emotional intent of text.  
2. **Toxicity Classification** – Detect threats, insults, or hate speech.  
3. **Machine Translation** – Automate translation between languages.  
4. **Named Entity Recognition (NER)** – Extract entities (names, places, orgs).  
5. **Spam Detection** – Identify unsolicited emails.  
6. **Grammatical Error Correction** – Correct grammar in text.  
7. **Topic Modeling** – Discover topics within document corpora (e.g., LDA).  
8. **Text Generation (NLG)** – Generate human-like text.  
9. **Autocomplete** – Predict next word or query (e.g., Google Search).  
10. **Chatbots** – Database query bots or conversation simulators (e.g., LaMDA).  
11. **Information Retrieval** – Retrieve relevant documents (e.g., Google Search).  
12. **Summarization** – Extractive or abstractive summarization.  
13. **Question Answering** – Multiple choice or open-domain QA (e.g., Watson).

## How Does NLP Work?

### Data Preprocessing
- **Stemming & Lemmatization** – Reduce words to root forms.  
- **Sentence Segmentation** – Break text into sentences.  
- **Stop Word Removal** – Remove common words (“the,” “a”).  
- **Tokenization** – Split text into words/tokens.

### Feature Extraction
- **Bag-of-Words (BoW)** – Word frequency counts.  
- **TF-IDF** – Importance-weighted word frequencies.  
- **Word2Vec** – Contextual embeddings via Skip-Gram/CBOW.  
- **GLoVE** – Embeddings using co-occurrence statistics.

### Modeling
- Traditional ML: Logistic regression, Naive Bayes, decision trees.  
- Deep learning: RNNs, CNNs, Transformers.  
- Language models predict the next word (Markov models, BERT, GPT).

## Six Important NLP Models

- **Eliza** (1960s): Early chatbot with rule-based replies.  
- **Tay** (2016): Microsoft chatbot that adopted harmful user language.  
- **BERT & Muppets family**: Contextual embeddings.  
- **GPT-3**: 175B parameter transformer model.  
- **LaMDA**: Google’s dialogue-based transformer chatbot.  
- **Mixture of Experts (MoE)**: Efficient routing with different parameters.

## Controversies Surrounding NLP

- **Stochastic Parrots (Bias & Scale)** – Large models absorb web biases.  
- **Coherence vs Sentience** – Misinterpretation of AI as conscious (e.g., LaMDA).  
- **Environmental Impact** – High energy and CO₂ emissions in training.  
- **High Costs** – Barriers for non-corporate researchers.  
- **Black Box Issue** – Lack of explainability in deep models.  
- **Criticism** – Sophisticated output misleads users into overestimating capabilities.
