## 3 — Bag of Words (BoW)

### What is Bag of Words?

**Bag of Words (BoW)** is a Natural Language Processing technique used for **text modeling** and **feature extraction**.  
It represents text as a collection (or "bag") of words and counts how many times each word appears in a document, while **ignoring grammar, order, and structure**.

It is called a "bag" because the order of words is discarded, and only the frequency of each word matters.

---

### Why is Bag of Words Used?

- Text data is **unstructured and variable in length**. Machine learning algorithms require **structured, fixed-length numerical vectors**.
- BoW converts text into **fixed-length numeric vectors**, making it suitable for ML models.
- It allows algorithms to work with **word frequencies** instead of raw text.
